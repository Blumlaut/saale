# Code of Conduct

The short version: this is a small project, and someone should be able to come here, ask a
question and get an answer without being made to feel stupid.

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, review comments, and anywhere else the project lives. What you do
elsewhere is your business, unless it follows someone into here.

## What is not okay

- Attacking a person instead of an argument, or dismissing someone because they are new to
  this.
- Harassment of any kind: unwanted attention, slurs, sexual content.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately derailing a thread, spamming, or picking a fight to enjoy it.
- Telling others to do any of the above.

## What is welcome

- Saying that something is wrong, confusing or badly explained, in a way that is about the
  code.
- "I don't understand this" is always a good question.
- Assuming the other person means well, and then asking.

## If something goes wrong

Tell me privately: [report it to
GitHub](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse), or send me a message on GitHub. I read
every report, and I tell you what I did about it.

What I do depends on the case: a note, a removed comment, a locked thread, a block. Anyone
asked to stop is expected to stop.

I make mistakes too. If I get it wrong, say so, and I'll look again.
